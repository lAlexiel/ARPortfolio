# Commission pricing references

Reviewed October 6, 2026. This is a small comparison of public artist price lists, not a market average or a prediction of sales. Different styles and levels of detail take different amounts of work.

- [AzulMagpie](https://azulmagpiecommission.carrd.co/) lists character sizes from $10 to $20 and charges backgrounds separately. This supports a low entry price for a deliberately simple drawing.
- [WhatsTheThymeStudios](https://whatsthethymestudios.com/commissions/) lists icons at $20–30, several half-body options around $25–50, and full-body character shots at $45–60. Its price tiers distinguish finishing and scope.

## Angie Rouge's published rates

| Framing | Simple shading | Full shading | Basic background add-on |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Headshot | $10 | $25 | +$10 |
| Half-body | $25 | $40 | +$15 |
| Full-body | $40 | $60 | +$20 |

Standalone backgrounds: $10 simple / $30 detailed. Detailed background add-on: +$30, replacing the basic background fee rather than stacking with it. Each extra character: 50% of the chosen character rate, minimum $10, yielding $10–30 for the current menu.

All prices are USD. The $10–60 range applies to the base character drawing; extras are separate and can make a combined commission cost more than $60. The final quote is agreed before work begins. Existing payment and copyright terms are unchanged.

These rates are adapted to the artist's requested range and should be reviewed against the time each drawing actually takes. Edit `content/commissions.json` to adjust them later.
